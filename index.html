Planetary Science

The Moon vs. Mars: Where Machines Rest

Why do machines last differently on the Moon and Mars? Compare their environments!

◌

The Moon

Airless & Preserved Forever
  • Gravity: 1/6th of Earth (You could leap 6 times higher!)
  • Atmosphere: Vacuum (No air, no wind, no weather, no rain).
  • Preservation: Zero erosion! Equipment, footprints, and tracks remain pristine for millions of years.
  • Temperature: Swings from -130°C (-202°F) at night to +120°C (248°F) in daylight.
  • Famous Equipment: Apollo 11 Descent Stage & Laser Ranging Retroreflector.
●

Mars

The Red Dusty Planet
  • Gravity: 3/8th of Earth (You could jump over a family car!)
  • Atmosphere: Thin carbon dioxide (~1% of Earth's air pressure).
  • Weather: Planet-encircling dust storms, dust devils, and cold winds.
  • Weathering: Fine iron oxide dust coats solar panels and gears over years.
  • Famous Equipment: Sojourner, Opportunity, Spirit, and Ingenuity.
